steps:
  - Many straight lines can separate the two classes.
  - For each line, measure the empty gap (the <b>margin</b>) to the nearest points.
  - Keep the line with the widest margin. The points touching the gap are the <b>support vectors</b>.
  - 'A setting called <b>C</b> decides how many points may sit inside the gap.'

Logistic regression finds a line that separates the classes. A Support Vector Machine finds the <mark>best-placed</mark> one: the line with the widest empty gap on either side. A wide gap means new points are less likely to fall on the wrong side.
