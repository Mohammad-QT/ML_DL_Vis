Imagine writing one review on each slip of paper. A model needs numbers, so give each word in your <mark>vocabulary</mark> its own column. For “cake and coffee” the counts for “cake” and “coffee” are both 1. For “coffee, coffee!” they are 0 and 2. This is called a <mark>bag of words</mark> because you keep what was in the review, but not its order.

Cleaning makes “Coffee!” and “coffee” match. Many common words add little information; we call them <mark>stop words</mark>. A <mark>stem</mark> is what remains after trimming an ending. The demo keeps “not”, because even short words can change the meaning. Ties when choosing the vocabulary are broken alphabetically.
