title: Apriori
lede: Find items people buy together. Then check whether one item makes the other any more likely.
figure: { caption: 'Two of these three baskets contain both marked items. The pair has support of two thirds.' }
steps_h: How Apriori works
steps:
  - Count each item. Keep only those that meet the minimum support.
  - Join frequent groups to make larger candidates. If any smaller subset failed, skip counting that candidate.
  - Scan the baskets for the remaining candidates. Repeat until no larger group can qualify.
  - Split each frequent group into a rule with two sides. Filter by confidence and sort by lift.
caution:
  h: High confidence can be misleading
  p: If 80% of all shoppers buy milk, a rule that predicts milk with 80% confidence is no better than that baseline. Its lift is 1. Even a high lift does not prove cause and effect.

    correct: 0

code: |
  import pandas as pd
  from mlxtend.preprocessing import TransactionEncoder
  from mlxtend.frequent_patterns import apriori, association_rules

  baskets = [['bread', 'milk'], ['bread', 'milk'], ['bread'], ['tea']]
  encoder = TransactionEncoder()
  encoded = encoder.fit(baskets).transform(baskets)
  table = pd.DataFrame(encoded, columns=encoder.columns_)
  groups = apriori(table, min_support=0.5, use_colnames=True)
  rules = association_rules(groups, metric='confidence', min_threshold=0.6)
  print(rules[['antecedents', 'consequents', 'support', 'confidence', 'lift']])
---
Imagine four baskets. Three contain bread, and two of those also contain milk. For the rule **bread → milk**, the <mark>support</mark> is the share of all baskets with both items: 2 out of 4. The <mark>confidence</mark> is the share of bread baskets that also have milk: 2 out of 3.

To get <mark>lift</mark>, divide the confidence by the overall share of baskets with milk. Here that is ½, so the lift is about 1.33. Lift above 1 means milk is more common in bread baskets than it is overall. Lift below 1 means it is less common.

Apriori saves work with one observation: any basket containing bread, milk and eggs must contain bread and milk. If the pair is too rare, the triple cannot pass the same support cutoff.
