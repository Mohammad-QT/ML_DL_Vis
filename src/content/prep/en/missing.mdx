Open any real table and some cells are empty: someone skipped a field, or a sensor failed. Scikit-learn models stop with an error when they meet a blank, so every blank needs a plan. There are two common ones.

- **Drop** the rows that have blanks. It is simple, but you lose data. With many blanks you can lose most of the table.
- **Fill** each blank with a typical value from its column: the <mark>mean</mark> (the average) or the **median** (the middle value). Both keep every row.

The median is safer when a column has an outlier, like one huge salary. The outlier pulls the mean up but hardly moves the median.
