A model can only do maths on numbers. A column like **Country** (France, Spain, Germany) has to be encoded first. There are two ways.

- **Label encoding** gives each category a number: France 0, Germany 1, Spain 2 (A to Z). It is one column and easy to read.
- **One-hot encoding** makes one column per category, with a 1 where the row belongs and 0 everywhere else.

The catch is that numbers have an order and a distance. With label encoding the model may read Spain (2) as bigger than France (0), and Germany as halfway between them. Countries have no such order, so for plain categories use <mark>one-hot</mark>. Label encoding is fine for the thing you predict (the labels, <code>y</code>) and for categories with a real order, such as small, medium and large.
