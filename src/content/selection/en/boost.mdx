A single shallow tree is weak: it draws a few straight cuts and misses most of the pattern. **Gradient boosting** turns many weak trees into a strong model by adding them one at a time.

Each round looks at where the model is still wrong and fits a new small tree to those errors. The new tree is added with a <mark>small weight</mark>, the learning rate, so no single tree can change the picture much. After many rounds the boundary becomes sharp.

**XGBoost** is a fast, carefully engineered version of this idea. It wins many table-data competitions. The Play tab shows the idea on two features, with a simplified version we wrote for this site. In the notebook you use the real library.
