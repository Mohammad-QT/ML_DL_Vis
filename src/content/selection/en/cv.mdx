So far you scored a model on one test set. That raises a fair worry: what if the test set happened to be easy, or unusually hard? The score would say more about your split than about your model.

**k-fold cross-validation** fixes this by using every point as test data exactly once. Cut the data into <mark>k folds</mark>. Train on k − 1 of them, test on the one left out, and repeat until each fold has had its turn. You get k scores. Their mean is your estimate, and their spread tells you how much to trust it.

A bigger k means more models to train, and each one sees more of the data. 5 and 10 are the usual choices. In the Play tab you can pick any of the seven classifiers and watch the held-out fold move.
