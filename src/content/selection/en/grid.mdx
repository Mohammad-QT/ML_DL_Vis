Most models have settings you choose before training: the <mark>hyperparameters</mark>. Kernel SVM has two. C says how hard the model tries to classify every training point. Gamma says how far a single point's influence reaches. Pick badly and the model is either too stiff or wildly bent.

**Grid search** picks them with brute force. List a few values for each setting, try every combination, and score each one with cross-validation. The best mean CV score wins.

Why not just use the training score? Because a bent enough model can fit the training points perfectly. The gap between the <mark>train score</mark> and the <mark>CV score</mark> is your overfitting meter. Look for the cell where the CV score is high and the gap is small.
