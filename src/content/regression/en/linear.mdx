steps:
  - Try a line through the points. Measure each point's miss, the vertical gap between the point and the line.
  - Square each miss so that gaps above and below cannot cancel, then add them up.
  - Tilt and shift the line until that total is as small as it can be. This is “least squares”.
  - Predict a new point by reading the line at its x.

Simple linear regression finds the <mark>best straight line</mark> through your points. The line has just two numbers: a <b>slope</b> (how much y changes when x goes up by one) and an <b>intercept</b> (where the line starts). Once you have them, predicting is a lookup: put in x, read off y.

Regression boards work like the classification boards, with one change: instead of a colour, each point has a number to predict. The filled points train the line. The ringed points are held back as the test, and <b>R²</b> scores both.
