A regression tree plays <mark>twenty questions</mark> with x. Each question cuts the x-axis in two, and in the end every piece predicts the <b>average</b> y of the training points that fell inside it. That gives a staircase.

There is no equation and no scaling to do. The tree simply finds where y jumps and puts a step there, so it is very good at data with sudden changes.
