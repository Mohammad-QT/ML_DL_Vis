A bendy curve can fit the training points closely but swing away from new points. <mark>Regularisation</mark> adds a cost for large weights. This gives the fit a reason to give up a little training accuracy if it can use smaller weights.

The board uses scaled Chebyshev polynomial features, a more numerically stable way to build a polynomial. Each bar shows the weight on one scaled feature, not the curve’s intercept. Both errors are <b>mean squared errors</b>: smaller is better.
