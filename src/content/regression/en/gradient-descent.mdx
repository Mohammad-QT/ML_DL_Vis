In linear regression, every slope and intercept gives a different line and a different error. Picture all those errors as heights on a hill map. <mark>Gradient descent</mark> starts at one spot and takes steps downhill. Close to the best line, the map levels out and the steps shrink.

This demo uses <b>batch</b> updates: each step checks every training point. The scikit-learn example uses stochastic gradient descent, which updates on one training point at a time.
